Boom Lift vs Scissor Lift: How to Choose the Right MEWP for Your UAE Project

By [Author name], [Role] · Published 12 Nov 2026 · Updated 20 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • Choose a scissor lift when you need to go straight up with a big platform and more people or materials.
  • Choose a boom lift when you must reach up and over obstacles or work far from the base.
  • Electric machines suit indoor work; diesel rough terrain machines suit outdoor sites.
  • Always pick a working height a little above your highest work point.

What's the difference?

A scissor lift raises its platform straight up on folding steel supports. A boom lift has an arm that reaches up and out, so the platform can move over and around obstacles. [Article text continues…]

Boom lift vs scissor lift at a glance

Scissor liftBoom lift
MovementStraight upUp and out
Working height7.8 – 18.2 m13.5 – 43 m
PlatformLarge, 2–4 peopleSmall, 1–2 people
Best forCeilings, fit-out, warehousesFacades, steel, hard-to-reach spots
