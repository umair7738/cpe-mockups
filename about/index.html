About us

Boom Lift & Scissor Lift Rental Company in Dubai

Cherry Pickers Equipment is an access equipment rental company based in Dubai Industrial City. We rent 30 models of scissor lifts and boom lifts, from 7.8 m to 43 m working height, to contractors and facility teams across all seven UAE emirates. We service every machine in our own yard, and we also buy and sell used access equipment.

A Dubai Team Focused on Safe Work at Height

Cherry Pickers Equipment is an access equipment company based in Saih Shuaib 4, Dubai Industrial City. We help contractors, facility managers and event teams reach high work safely, with the right machine delivered to site and a team that answers the phone.

Our fleet of 30 scissor lift and boom lift models is maintained by our own technicians, and our sales team trades used JLG and Genie machines with buyers in the UAE, India and Vietnam.

Our story

Built Around Work at Height

Our business was built on sourcing and supplying used access equipment. We buy boom lifts and scissor lifts from trusted, reputed rental fleets in 27+ countries – machines with a strong maintenance history – and supply them to buyers in India, Vietnam and 10+ other countries.

With over 700 machines supplied and 95+ customers, we are now bringing that experience to rental in the UAE: a relatively young fleet of diesel, electric and hybrid machines, maintained to OEM guidelines by qualified, trained engineers, with a fast response to breakdown calls.

Today one team covers the whole life of a machine – rental, used and new equipment sales, AMC and repairs, and safety support.
