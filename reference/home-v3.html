Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about hiring, delivery, servicing and buying used machines.

Still have a question? WhatsApp us
What access equipment can I rent from you?

We rent 30 models of electric and rough terrain scissor lifts and electric, articulated and telescopic boom lifts, from 7.8 m to 43 m working height.

What is the minimum rental period?

We rent for short projects and long contracts – from a few days to several weeks or longer. Share your dates and we'll quote for that period.

Do you deliver outside Dubai?

Yes – we deliver and collect across all seven emirates, as soon as possible.

Do you supply operators?

Yes, operators are available on request – mention it when you ask for a quote. If your own team operates the machine, they need recognised training such as an IPAF PAL card.

What do I get with a rental machine?

A maintained machine inspected before hire, with both platform and ground controls, delivery and collection arranged by our team, and 24/7 breakdown support on +971 56 801 9344.

Which brands do you rent and sell?

JLG, Genie and Dingli are our core brands, along with Haulotte, Snorkel, Nifty and Sinoboom.

Can you service machines that we own?

Yes – we offer AMC (annual maintenance contracts), on-site repairs and workshop services for boom lifts and scissor lifts.

Can I check a used machine before buying?

Yes. Every listing shows the year, origin, specs and condition photos, and you can ask for inspection reports and documents before you buy.

Can you export a machine to my country?

Yes. We handle packing, logistics and shipping documents – 750+ machines have gone to India and 100+ to Vietnam so far.
