How to Choose the Right Electric Boom Lift

Outreach: Check how far you need to reach sideways, not only up – our electric booms reach 6.1 m to 13.5 m out.

Floor loading: Booms are heavier than scissors (3.9 – 7.8 tonnes). Confirm the floor can take the machine.
