Best Uses for Articulated Boom Lifts

  • Piping and MEP work over obstacles
  • Steel structures and roof work
  • Tanks, plants and industrial facilities
  • Building maintenance and repairs
  • Work behind or above other equipment

How to Choose the Right Articulated Boom Lift

Up-and-over clearance: Articulated booms bend over obstacles. If nothing is in the way, a telescopic boom may reach further.

Outreach: Our articulated booms reach 7.6 m to 21.3 m sideways.

Ground: Diesel 4x4 models work on rough sites; check slope and ground bearing.

Height: From 15.7 m for everyday work up to 43.15 m with the Genie Z-135/70.

Articulated Boom Lift Rental Questions

What is the difference between an articulated and a telescopic boom lift?

An articulated boom has joints that bend up and over obstacles. A telescopic boom extends straight out for maximum reach. Choose articulated when something is in the way.

What is the tallest articulated boom you rent?

The Genie Z-135/70, with a 43.15 m working height and 21.25 m outreach.

Do you supply an operator?

Yes, on request. Tell us when you book.
