Quick guide

Which Boom Lift Do I Need?

Choose telescopic boom lifts when…

  • You need the longest straight reach from one position
  • Nothing is in the way between the machine and the work
  • Facades, steel erection, bridges and large structures
View 7 telescopic models →

Choose articulated boom lifts when…

  • Obstacles are in the way – the arm bends up and over
  • You need tight spots behind or above other equipment
  • Piping, MEP, plants and roof work
View 10 articulated models →

Choose electric boom lifts when…

  • You work indoors or in noise-sensitive areas
  • No exhaust is allowed – malls, airports, atriums
  • Night work and occupied buildings
View 3 electric models →

Best Uses for Boom Lifts

  • Facades, glazing and cladding
  • Steel structures and roof work
  • Piping and MEP work over obstacles
  • Atriums, malls and airport terminals
  • Industrial plants, tanks and yards

Questions

Boom Lift Rental Questions

What is a boom lift?

A boom lift (also called a cherry picker) is a work platform on a telescopic or jointed arm, mounted on a wheeled base. It reaches up and out, so you can work away from the machine or over obstacles.

Telescopic, articulated or electric – which boom lift do I need?

Telescopic for the longest straight reach, articulated when you need to go up and over obstacles, and electric for indoor or noise-sensitive work with no exhaust.

What is the tallest boom lift you rent?

The Genie Z-135/70 articulated boom, with a 43.15 m working height and 21.25 m outreach.

Do you supply an operator?

Yes, on request. Tell us when you book.
