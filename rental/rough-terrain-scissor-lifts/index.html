How to Choose the Right Rough Terrain Scissor Lift

Ground conditions: Rough terrain models handle compacted soil, gravel and slopes – check the site gradient before booking.

Deck size and load: Large decks carry crews, panels and tools together – up to 680 kg on our machines.

Working height: Our rough terrain scissors reach 15.1 m to 18.2 m.

Access and transport: These machines are large and heavy (5.9 – 7.5 tonnes) – confirm site access for delivery.

Rough Terrain Scissor Lift Rental Questions

Can a rough terrain scissor lift be used indoors?

They run on diesel, so they are not suited to enclosed spaces without proper ventilation. Indoors, use an electric scissor lift.

How much weight can a rough terrain scissor lift carry?

Our Genie GS-4390 RT and GS-5390 RT carry up to 680 kg – people, tools and materials together.

What is the difference between a rough terrain and an electric scissor lift?

Rough terrain models are diesel, 4x4 and built for outdoor ground. Electric models are quiet and compact, for smooth indoor floors.
