Quick guide

Scissor Lift, Boom Lift or Spider Lift?

Choose a scissor lift when…

  • You need to go straight up
  • Several people, tools or materials go on the platform
  • The work area is directly above the machine
  • Indoors on smooth floors (slab electric) or outdoors on rough ground (rough terrain electric, diesel 4x4)

All scissor lifts →

Slab Electric →   Rough Terrain Electric →   Diesel 4x4 →

Choose a boom lift when…

  • You need to reach up and out, away from the base
  • Obstacles are in the way (articulated)
  • You need the longest straight reach (telescopic)
  • Quiet, zero-emission work indoors (electric boom)

All boom lifts →

Telescopic →   Articulated →   Electric →

Choose a spider lift when…

  • Access is tight – narrow doors, gates or corridors
  • Floors are delicate or the ground is soft (light weight, non-marking tracks)
  • You set up on slopes, steps or uneven ground (outriggers level the machine)
  • Atriums, lobbies, glazing and tree work, indoors or outdoors

FAQ

Equipment Rental Questions

What access equipment can I rent from Cherry Pickers Equipment?

We rent 30 models of boom lifts (telescopic, articulated and electric) and scissor lifts (slab electric, rough terrain electric and diesel 4x4), from 7.8 m to 43 m working height. Spider lifts from 17 m to 36 m are coming soon.

What is a spider lift and when do I need one?

A spider lift is a compact boom on rubber tracks with outriggers. It fits through narrow access, is light on delicate floors and levels itself on slopes, stairs or soft ground. Choose one when access is tight or the floor cannot take a standard boom.

What is the minimum rental period?

You can rent for a single day. We also offer weekly, monthly and long-term hire.

Do you deliver outside Dubai?

Yes. We deliver and collect across all seven emirates: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Umm Al Quwain.

Do you supply operators?

Yes, on request. Tell us when you book and we will include an operator with the machine.

How do I choose between a scissor lift and a boom lift?

Choose a scissor lift to go straight up with a large platform. Choose a boom lift when you need to reach up and out, or over obstacles.
