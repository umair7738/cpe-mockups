Best Uses for Telescopic Boom Lifts

  • Facades, glazing and cladding
  • Steel erection and large-span structures
  • Bridges and infrastructure
  • Signage and high-level installation
  • Tree work and outdoor maintenance

How to Choose the Right Telescopic Boom Lift

Reach vs height: Telescopic booms give the longest outreach – up to 23.3 m on the Genie S-85.

Platform load: Need more on the platform? The Genie S-80X carries 454 kg; JLG 660SJ and 860SJ carry 340 kg.

Clear path: A straight boom needs a clear line to the work point. If obstacles are in the way, use an articulated boom.

Ground and access: These are heavy machines (6.9 – 18.1 tonnes) – check ground bearing and site access.

Telescopic Boom Lift Rental Questions

Which telescopic boom has the longest reach?

The Genie S-85, with 23.32 m horizontal outreach and 27.9 m working height.

Which telescopic boom carries the most weight?

The Genie S-80X, with a 454 kg platform capacity.

Telescopic or articulated – which do I need?

Telescopic for the longest straight reach; articulated when you need to go up and over obstacles.
