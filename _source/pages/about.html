Our story

Built Around Work at Height

Our business was built on sourcing and supplying used access equipment. We buy boom lifts and scissor lifts from trusted, reputed rental fleets in 27+ countries – machines with a strong maintenance history – and supply them to buyers in India, Vietnam and 10+ other countries.

With over 700 machines supplied and 95+ customers, we are now bringing that experience to rental in the UAE: a relatively young fleet of diesel, electric and hybrid machines, maintained to OEM guidelines by qualified, trained engineers, with a fast response to breakdown calls.

Today one team covers the whole life of a machine – rental, used and new equipment sales, AMC and repairs, and safety support.
