Who we are

A Dubai Team Focused on Safe Work at Height

Cherry Pickers Equipment is an access equipment company based in Saih Shuaib 4, Dubai Industrial City. We help contractors, facility managers and event teams reach high work safely, with the right machine delivered to site and a team that answers the phone.

Our fleet of 30 scissor lift and boom lift models is maintained by our own technicians, and our sales team trades used JLG and Genie machines with buyers in the UAE, India and Vietnam.

  • Based in Dubai Industrial City, serving all seven emirates
  • Own workshop for servicing and repairs
  • Rental, maintenance and used equipment sales from one team
