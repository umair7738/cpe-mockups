2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you send an enquiry, ask for a quote or contact us, you may give us:

  • Your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address and company name
  • The type of enquiry (rental, sales or service) and the machine, model or category you are interested in

Information we collect automatically

  • Device and browser information, such as browser type, operating system, screen size and an approximate location based on your IP address

Information from others

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Reply to your enquiries and prepare quotes
  • Deliver rental, maintenance, repair and sales services, including delivery and collection
  • Prepare and send invoices, contracts and other documents
  • Plan site safety and arrange operator details when you ask for an operator
  • Improve our website and understand which pages and machines people look at
  • Measure how well our adverts work
  • Meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations

We only use your information when we have a reason to:

  • Your request – you asked us for a quote, information or a call back
  • Contract – we need it to provide a rental, service or sale you have agreed to
  • Legitimate interest – running and improving our business, for example measuring our website and adverts, in a way that does not override your rights
  • Legal obligation – for example keeping financial records
  • Consent – for marketing messages. You can withdraw consent at any time.

[Confirm tools in use.] We may use the following tools on this website:
